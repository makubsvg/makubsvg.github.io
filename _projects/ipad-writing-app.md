An app for taking handwritten notes.
It is written in swift and using swiftui as well as metal with metalkit.
The app features my own custom line renderer for drawing the lines strokes.
The renderer supports rounded joins and ends.
Moreover, the lines are drawn with a shader that antialiases them with a smooth edge to transparency transition.
